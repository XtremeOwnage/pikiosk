    Views

    Named web pages and camera streams. MQTT, the API and Home Assistant pick views by name. Switch a view off to skip it in every rotation; it can still be shown directly.

      Rotations

      A rotation shows views in turn. Showing something for a set time pauses it, and it carries on afterwards; showing something with no time stops it until resumed.

      No rotations yet.

      Add views first, then a rotation to cycle through them.

      Sound & cast

      Sounds, volume, and receiving media from phones and Home Assistant.

      Playback

      Casting

      iPhones, iPads and Macs on the same network can mirror their screen or play video and music here (Control Center → Screen Mirroring). Android phones and Home Assistant send media over DLNA: the kiosk shows up as a DLNA Digital Media Renderer.

        Home Assistant discovery

        The kiosk publishes one retained device message, which Home Assistant 2024.12 or newer turns into a device with all its entities:

        Clearing removes the device from Home Assistant and deletes the retained state, sound list and availability. They come back with Republish or the next reconnect.
